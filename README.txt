MECH EQUIP INTERNATIONAL — WEBSITE TEMPLATE

Files:
- index.html
- assets/style.css
- assets/script.js
- assets/logo.png

How to use:
1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. Edit company phone, email, address, product names and text directly in index.html.
3. Replace the sample Unsplash image URLs in assets/style.css if you have your own equipment photos.
4. The enquiry form is currently front-end demo only. Connect it to your PHP/Node/API/email service before launch.
5. Upload the full folder to your hosting/public_html directory.

Design direction:
- Professional heavy-equipment / infrastructure company
- Responsive mobile/tablet/desktop layout
- Sticky navigation
- Hero, About, Product Offerings, Services, Industries, Inquiry, Footer
- Scroll reveal animation and animated counters
