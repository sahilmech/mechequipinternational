High-Performance Jaw Crushers for Primary Crushing

Jaw Crusher Series

Our Heavy-Duty Jaw Crushers are engineered for the toughest primary crushing applications. Designed to handle large feed sizes of hard, abrasive materials, they offer unparalleled reliability and high capacity. By utilizing a deep crushing chamber and optimized toggle kinematics, our jaw crushers guarantee maximum throughput and a long service life, making them the ultimate primary reduction solution for mining, quarrying, and recycling operations.

They are built as a primary reduction solution where large rock and ore must be brought down to a size the rest of the circuit can handle.

Why Choose Our Equipment? (Key Benefits)

Cost-Effective Operation: High capacity with low energy consumption.
Ease of Maintenance: Accessible components and simple wear part replacement.
Reliability: Proven performance even with the hardest granite, basalt, and iron ore.

Key Features

  • Robust heavy-duty cast steel frame ensuring durability in extreme conditions.
  • Deep, symmetrical crushing chamber for maximized feed acceptance and high throughput.
  • Easy, hydraulic adjustment of the Closed Side Setting (CSS) to control product size.
  • High-quality forged steel eccentric shafts and premium heavy-duty spherical roller bearings.
  • Reversible jaw plates manufactured from high-manganese steel for extended wear life.
