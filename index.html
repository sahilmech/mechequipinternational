ABOUT OUR RANGE

Equipment for concrete, aggregate and mining work.

The range follows one hierarchy: construction machinery for batching and storage, road machinery for crushing, screening and washing, and mining machinery for excavation and mine crushing.

Mobile, compact and stationary plants produce concrete on site. Cement and flyash silos store powdered materials. Jaw, cone and roll crushers, vibrating screens and washing systems prepare aggregate. Excavators and propell crushers support the mining face.
