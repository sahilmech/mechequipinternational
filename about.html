ABOUT US

Machinery for construction, roads and mining.

Mech Equip International supplies equipment across three groups: construction machinery for concrete production and storage, road machinery for crushing, screening and washing, and mining machinery for excavation and mine crushing.

Concrete mixing plants remain central where India’s civil works need the right quality and quantity of concrete on time. The same projects also need clean, graded aggregate — which is where jaw crushers, cone crushers, roll crushers, vibrating screens and washing systems come in.
