Propell Crusher

Propell Crusher

The propell crusher is the mining-machinery crusher in this range, paired with the excavator for operations that break ore and rock before screening, washing or further beneficiation.

It is intended for mine and quarry circuits where run-of-mine material has to be reduced to a size the downstream plant can accept. Final selection depends on feed size, abrasiveness, required product and how the crusher sits next to the excavator and any road-machinery screens already on site.

Typical duties

  • Size reduction of ore and quarry rock in a mining circuit.
  • Preparing feed for vibrating screens and washing systems.
  • Supporting aggregate supply into concrete and road works.
